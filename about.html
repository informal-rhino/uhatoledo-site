What We Do

Bringing preventative and educative healthcare services to underprivileged persons within Lucas County and the Greater Toledo Area.

Universal Health Aid Toledo’s focus is to bring preventative and educative healthcare services to underprivileged persons within Lucas County and the Greater Toledo Area. With about a quarter of the region’s population of 431,000 individuals living below the poverty level, access to a healthy lifestyle and quality care is greatly limited for many. As such, UHA Toledo proves to be a powerful facilitator in bringing comprehensive and holistic service to an extremely intimate level.

2016’s Free Health Fair saw about 100 children complete their annual school and sports physicals, an errand that would otherwise come at an expense to the impoverished local families, noted as having an average income of $26,680. In providing access to such medical specialties as pediatrics, dentistry, family medicine, ophthalmology, and cardiology, UHA Toledo strives to also offer a sense of comfort and unification to the community.

Connecting patients with physicians with an array of resident health networks, UHA Toledo looks to strengthen the relationship and trust between the disadvantaged and healthcare professionals by means of free health screenings and informative service. The volunteers and sponsors of UHA Toledo are committed more than ever to the goal of helping and serving all.

Outreach

Forming and maintaining relationships with local institutions is key to the success of UHA Toledo. As such, we are extremely proud of and thankful for the continued support of the following organizations:

  • Toledo Public Library
  • Seagate Foodbank
  • Zepf Wellness Center
  • Rosary Cathedral
  • Maumee Valley Country Day School
  • And so many more that help to make our services available!

Workshops

UHA Toledo Team Members undergo extensive training in an effort to cultivate a professional work environment not only within our organization, but in communication with other facilities. By providing in-house seminars as well as bringing in guest speakers, we seek to develop our volunteers into active members of the healthcare field. These seminars include:

  • Professional Correspondence: Effective Speaking and Writing
  • Health Screening Sensitivity Training
  • The Steve North Workshop, provided by Steve North, founder of LifeLine Toledo and the 4.5 Poverty Immersion Program

Communication

Effective and significant cooperation is essential for the smooth organization of our programs. To that extent, each of our volunteers are versed in the most collaborative technologies as a means of keeping everyone on task and informed throughout the planning processes. These tools include, but are not limited to:

  • Google Drive
  • Slack
  • Trello
