Universal Health Aid · Toledo

Free health care for the Greater Toledo Area.

UHA Toledo is committed to providing premier healthcare service to the Greater Toledo Area. Since 2013, our volunteers have brought free health screenings, preventive care, and health education to Lucas County.

Services offered

Helping all, serving all.

Our annual Free Health Fair provides access to numerous medical specialties, from internal medicine and pediatrics to cardiology, along with connection to follow-up care, prescription disposal bags, opioid education, and free meals.

COVID-19 Update

In lieu of the COVID-19 pandemic, UHA Toledo redirected its efforts to focus on helping the Greater Toledo Area obtain relevant health information, household necessities, and personal protective equipment (PPE). We wrapped up with our Final Grocery & PPE Packaging Event, and we are still dedicated to ensuring that the Toledo community has all of its health-related needs met.
